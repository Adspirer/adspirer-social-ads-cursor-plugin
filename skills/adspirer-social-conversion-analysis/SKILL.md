# Conversion funnel analysis

Load adspirer-social-ads-tools. Inspect conversion_funnel_analysis and audit_conversion_tracking
schemas. Select the relevant Meta/TikTok account, period, and business conversion.
Read evidence in order: tracking, expectations, traffic, conversion, and cost economics
where available. Distinguish unknown or not-assessed stages from passed or failed ones.
Do not infer landing-page contents without reading them through an authorized source.
Do not promise complete attribution, individual customer journeys, or access to GA4/GTM here.
Return the earliest supported problem, evidence, confidence, and next diagnostic step.
This is read-only; changes need a separate proposed plan and explicit approval.
